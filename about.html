        The Producers of Suffering, or TPOS for short, is a website/article made by a 17 year old plague inc
        fan. 
        Makers Note: Its crazy, how theres a whole different world living with us, sometimes living right under 
        our noses, and sometimes how the creatures living in this world decide to attempt to put some of us to 
        sleep forever. Everyday is technically a fight between us, and them, will they overpower you and
        make you sick, or will you overpower them and survive another day, sick-free?
